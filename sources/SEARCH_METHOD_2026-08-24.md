# Bounded literature-search record

Search date: 2026-08-24.

## Question and scope

The search had three bounded purposes: (1) verify the two publications supplying the aggregate heart and kidney nitrite values; (2) look for a publicly indexed application of partial-identification or linear-inverse-problem results to partition bulk nitrite/NOx between eNOS/NOS3 and iNOS/NOS2 sources; and (3) look for a report combining the locked male-Swiss-albino/lead-nitrate/high-dose-ASEO model with same-animal vascular-function, NOS, renal-injury, and tissue-lead measurements. It was not a systematic review, citation-index analysis, or priority determination.

   ```text
   "lead nitrate"[Title/Abstract] AND
   ("garlic essential oil"[Title/Abstract] OR "Allium sativum essential oil"[Title/Abstract]) AND
   (nitrite[Title/Abstract] OR "nitric oxide"[Title/Abstract] OR eNOS[Title/Abstract]
   ```


Exact-title and DOI lookups were also run in PubMed, PMC, Crossref/DOI resolver, and publisher search pages for `10.1016/j.toxrep.2025.101950` and `10.18311/ti/2024/v31i2/35881`. Citation and reference links from the located records were followed for measurement and mechanism papers. General web searches repeated the exact phrases `partial identification`, `inverse problem`, `row space`, and `identifiability` with `bulk nitrite`, `NOx`, `eNOS`, `iNOS`, `NOS2`, `NOS3`, `lead nitrate`, and `garlic essential oil` to catch terminology not indexed in abstracts.

## Screening and record limitations

Titles and available abstracts returned by these focused queries were screened for the two target source papers, a genuinely source-resolved measurement, or an application of the stated identification framework to NOS-source partition. Full text was checked when publicly accessible and materially relevant. Original animal/cell studies, original assay/method papers, reporting guidance, and the two publications underlying the aggregate values received priority. Commercial summaries, unsourced tertiary pages, unrelated garlic preparations, and papers that did not constrain the measurement or interpretation were excluded.

The web interfaces were queried interactively and their exact result counts and export files were not archived; no numerical hit count is therefore claimed. Screening was focused rather than exhaustive; dual-reviewer screening and formal deduplication were not performed. Database indexing, query parsing, and records may change after the search date. Absence from these results is not evidence that no related work exists.

## Bounded conclusion

The searches located the known cardiac and renal aggregate-report publications and related lead/garlic studies, but did not locate a publicly indexed paper that applies the manuscript's linear partial-identification/row-space analysis specifically to partition bulk nitrite or NOx between NOS sources. They also did not locate a public report combining the locked model with same-animal aortic function, eNOS coupling/cGMP, renal NOS2/nitration/injury, and tissue lead. The two source publications provide no public cross-publication animal identifiers or linkage key, so cross-organ pairing cannot be established from the public record. These are narrow, date-bounded search observations only; they are not claims of scientific priority, exhaustive novelty, or nonexistence.
